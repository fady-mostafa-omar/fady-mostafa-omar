# Fady Mostafa Omar

Mechatronics engineer working on **EEG-based brain–computer interfaces for neurorehabilitation** and **biosignal machine learning (EEG/ECG)**.

## Education
- **M.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering**, Military Technical College, Cairo
  Thesis: *Development of a Smart Robotic System Integrating Artificial Intelligence and Brain Computer Interface for Neurorehabilitation in Stroke Patients*
- **B.Sc. in Mechatronics Engineering**, Helwan University

## Research interests
EEG decoding · motor BCIs · neurorehabilitation robotics · ECG analysis · reproducible, leakage-safe evaluation of biosignal ML

## Repositories

**EEG and BCI**
| Repository | What it is |
|---|---|
| [movement-library-calibration](https://github.com/fady-mostafa-omar/movement-library-calibration) | How much calibration does a library of other people's executed movements replace? A controlled EEG study on 15 people (leave-one-subject-out), with all code, per-person results and dated analysis protocols. *Manuscript under review.* |
| [realtime-bci-hand-exoskeleton](https://github.com/fady-mostafa-omar/realtime-bci-hand-exoskeleton) | M.Sc. project: a real-time EEG BCI on a Jetson Nano that drives a 3D-printed hand exoskeleton, tested online with five volunteers. *IEEE NILES 2025.* |
| [eeg-prosthetic-arm](https://github.com/fady-mostafa-omar/eeg-prosthetic-arm) | B.Sc. project: EEG decoding (CSP, wavelet scalograms, VGG16) that drives a 3D-printed prosthetic arm. *IUGRC 2022.* |

**ECG and heart sounds**
| Repository | What it is |
|---|---|
| [ecg-arrhythmia-detector](https://github.com/fady-mostafa-omar/ecg-arrhythmia-detector) | Finds every heartbeat in a single-lead ECG and sorts it into the five AAMI classes; tested on 22 patients the model never saw. |
| [ecg-af-detector](https://github.com/fady-mostafa-omar/ecg-af-detector) | Detects atrial fibrillation and its burden in a single-lead ECG; tested on Holter recordings from 45 patients the model never saw. |
| [ecg-lead2-diagnosis](https://github.com/fady-mostafa-omar/ecg-lead2-diagnosis) | How much of a 12-lead ECG diagnosis survives in lead II alone (PTB-XL), under real recorded noise, with an external test on a hospital in a second country. |
| [single-lead-ecg-analyzer](https://github.com/fady-mostafa-omar/single-lead-ecg-analyzer) | Holter-style analysis of one ECG lead, from 10 seconds to 24 hours, combining the three projects above into a PDF report. |
| [digital-smart-stethoscope](https://github.com/fady-mostafa-omar/digital-smart-stethoscope) | Heart-sound phases, murmur detection and recording quality (CirCor DigiScope, tested on PhysioNet/CinC 2016 without retraining). Research prototype, not a medical device. |
